HOW TO PLAY

Controls

  • Move: WASD or arrow keys.
  • Shoot: hold Space. On touchscreens, touch/drag on the game to move and fire.
  • Aim by moving; shots travel upward.

Enemies and endless levels

  • Avoid colliding with neon rings and enemy shots. Orange rings shoot at you.
  • Survive and destroy enemies to earn coins. A boss arrives every 100 points.
  • The huge boss pulls you toward it and fires spreads of fireballs. Keep moving and shoot its core.
  • Defeat the boss to clear the level, earn bonus coins, and choose one ability card. The next level is harder; levels continue endlessly.

Ability cards (for this run)

  • Rapid Fire: shorter delay between shots.
  • Triple Shot: adds two angled bolts to each shot.
  • Extra Shield: blocks one collision or projectile hit.
  • Turbo Engine: increases movement speed and reduces the boss's pull.

Permanent shop upgrades

  • Rapid Fire: start every run with a faster firing rate.
  • Double Shot: start with a wider multi-shot spread.
  • Starting Shield: begin each run with extra protection.
  • Improved Engine: start faster and resist the boss's pull.

Coins and shop purchases are saved in this browser on this device. Global scores can be enabled with Supabase credentials.
